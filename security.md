## Is this a honeypot?

Risk scanners that inspect Token-2022 transfer hooks can flag a Trucee token as non-transferable or "restricted" before graduation. The flag is correct about the mechanics: before graduation, the token cannot be sent wallet to wallet or traded through DBC directly or through an aggregator. Such attempts fail in the hook with `Pre-graduation transfers must go through the Zero-PvP router`. This is by design. If tokens could move outside the router, they could be sold to other buyers at a profit, which is the PvP that Trucee removes before graduation.

A honeypot traps your money. Trucee does not:

1. **Trucee never blocks an exit.** Before graduation, any holder can call the router's `sell` on their own position ([programs/zpvp_router/src/lib.rs](https://github.com/trucee-fun/trucee-programs/blob/main/programs/zpvp_router/src/lib.rs), `sell` → `exit_at_cost`). The router sells the tokens into the DBC curve, puts the proceeds in the market's buffer and refunds the holder's cost basis, minus the trading fee at cost and a 2-lamport haircut ([programs/zpvp_router/src/math.rs](https://github.com/trucee-fun/trucee-programs/blob/main/programs/zpvp_router/src/math.rs), `refund_for`). The router has no admin key, pause switch or blocklist. Beyond account validation, the router checks two things on a `sell`: the amount must be above zero and within the position (error 6007), and the buffer must cover the refund (error 6015). The solvency fuzz below never produced a buffer short of a refund. On xStock-quoted markets, an outside party can still pause exits: the xStock issuer (see [Known limitations](#known-limitations)).
2. **The hook goes away at graduation, and Trucee has no say in it.** DBC itself revokes the transfer hook in the swap that completes the curve (DBC [process_swap.rs](https://github.com/MeteoraAg/dynamic-bonding-curve/blob/main/programs/dynamic-bonding-curve/src/instructions/swap/process_swap.rs), cited in [programs/zpvp_hook/src/lib.rs](https://github.com/trucee-fun/trucee-programs/blob/main/programs/zpvp_hook/src/lib.rs)). From then on the token is a plain Token-2022 token. The end-to-end suite checks this on every run: "DBC revoked the transfer hook at graduation" and "after graduation: plain wallet transfer works (token is free)" ([src/zpvp_e2e.ts](https://github.com/trucee-fun/trucee-litesvm-diff/blob/main/src/zpvp_e2e.ts)).
3. **Upgrade authority is disclosed.** Who can change the program code is listed in the next section.

What you still have to trust is the upgrade authority. An upgrade could change any of these rules, including the hook. That is why the key is disclosed below and listed again under [Known limitations](#known-limitations).

## Known limitations

1. **Tokens cannot be transferred before graduation, by design.** Wallet sends, aggregator routes, and use in other protocols (lending, LP) all fail until the curve completes. Scanners may flag the token for this. A variant where transfers carry the cost basis along was researched and not built. Trustless OTC through a Jito bundle with an assertion program cannot be blocked on-chain, and that holds for any design that allows transfers before graduation, including a router "transfer with basis". Wallet-Native Basis is a roadmap item, not a shipped feature.
2. **not independently reviewed, upgradeable, team-held key.** The upgrade authority listed above can replace either program. The mainnet-beta caps (item 5) limit how much a single curve can hold. They do not remove this trust.
3. **Sybil wallets and the Anti-Whale cap.** The cap applies to one position, which belongs to one wallet. One person can buy from many wallets, and the program cannot tell. Each of those wallets still buys up the same curve. The cap limits how much one wallet can hold, not how much one person can hold.
4. **xStock issuers keep their powers.** xStock quote mints (TSLAx, NVDAx) have the permanent-delegate and pausable extensions, so the issuer can pause transfers or claw back balances ( `QUOTE_CAPS` comment in [programs/zpvp_router/src/lib.rs](https://github.com/trucee-fun/trucee-programs/blob/main/programs/zpvp_router/src/lib.rs)). Claw-backs can reach a market's buffer vault. While a quote mint is paused, buys, exits and claims on that market cannot settle. xStock markets are tested in LiteSVM with mainnet snapshots only, because devnet has no xStock mints.
5. **Mainnet-beta caps.** The default build accepts only these quote mints and graduation thresholds: wSOL up to 10 SOL, TSLAx up to 5 shares, NVDAx up to 10 shares (`QUOTE_CAPS`). Changing them requires a program upgrade.
6. **Exit at cost is not zero-loss.** Before graduation, an exit gives back the basis minus the trading fee at cost, a 2-lamport haircut and the 16 lamports withheld per buy. Network fees and account rent are separate. A "Zero-Loss Exit" is a roadmap item.
7. **Auto-Exit uses a standing approval.** To use Auto-Exit, the SDK approves the market PDA as delegate for `u64::MAX` of your base tokens. The unlimited amount covers tokens bought later in the same market without needing a fresh approval. Before graduation, only the router can move the base token; wallet-to-wallet transfers, including sending extra tokens to another holder, are hook-blocked. In the current code, the approval is used only in `auto_exit`, after your deadline and while the curve is live. It stays on your token account after an exit and after graduation until you revoke it ([Router v5 review](security/router-v5-review.md), A2). The web app's trade panel offers **Revoke approval** once no deadline is active, or once the market is finalized; it sends one Token-2022 revoke ([src/lib/trade/build-auto-exit.ts](https://github.com/trucee-fun/trucee-app/blob/main/src/lib/trade/build-auto-exit.ts), `buildRevokeApprovalTx`).
8. **No PvP guarantee after graduation.** Exit at cost ends when the curve completes: DBC rejects swaps on a completed curve, and `auto_exit` returns 6021. The token then trades freely once the pool has migrated to DAMM v2, at market prices. Meteora's mainnet keepers migrate SOL pools only when the graduation threshold is 10 SOL or more , so Trucee's crank runs the migration for smaller pools. If the crank is down, the pool waits until someone sends DBC's permissionless migration instruction.
9. **Market opening is permissionless:** anyone can open the market for a pool; the app handles an already-open market. `init_market` and the hook's extra-account list take no creator signature, so a third party can open the market between the creator's pool and market transactions. Every Market field comes from the pool and its config, so that market is the same one the creator would have opened, and no funds are at risk. The creator's own market transaction then fails with "account already in use". The SDK's `buildLaunchPlan`, given the first plan's config and mint keypairs, skips every account that already exists, and the web app rebuilds the launch once that way. If the creator's initial buy is still unsent, the app first shows a fresh quote and asks before sending it ( [src/launch.ts](https://github.com/trucee-fun/trucee-sdk/blob/main/src/launch.ts); [src/lib/launch/run-launch.ts](https://github.com/trucee-fun/trucee-app/blob/main/src/lib/launch/run-launch.ts)). See [For creators → If a launch is interrupted](creators.md#if-a-launch-is-interrupted).
